### Featured Research & Publications

* **CORE-FGO: Co-Optimized Reliable and Energy-Aware Factor Graph Optimization for Fuzzy-Based Autonomous Vehicle Localization**  
  *Erfan Shabgir and Amir Mahdi Hosseini Monazzah*  
  Submitted to *IEEE Transactions on Emerging Topics in Computing (TETC)*, 2026.  
  * **Overview:** Designed a federated three-layer localization hierarchy decoupling tightly-coupled local Dead Reckoning (IMU, wheel encoders, 2-DoF LTV bicycle dynamics) and Map-Matching (RGB-D VO, DVFS 3D LiDAR) graphs from a global Master Factor Graph, supervised by dual Mamdani fuzzy engines and calibrated offline via Optuna and NSGA-II.  
  * **Code & Benchmarks:** [CPS-Lab-IUST/Localization](https://github.com/CPS-Lab-IUST/Localization)
